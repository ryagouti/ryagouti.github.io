About

PhD Candidate in Machine Learning
Inria Lille · Scool Team

I am a PhD candidate in the Scool team - Inria Lille at the Université de Lille , supervised by Emilie Kaufmann and Rémy Degenne .

My research focuses on adaptive testing and allocation strategies for clinical trials. I am particularly interested in multi-armed bandits, sequential hypothesis testing, adaptive experimental design, and asymptotic statistics.
